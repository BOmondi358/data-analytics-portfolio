# Project 1: Excel Business Performance Analysis - Chocolate Sales

## Overview

This project analyzes chocolate sales performance from January to August 2022 to provide management with clear visibility into revenue trends, sales volume, product performance, market contribution, and salesperson effectiveness. The analysis is designed to simulate a real-world business reporting scenario where decision-makers require concise, data-driven insights supported by interactive dashboards.

The project demonstrates my ability to clean and analyze transactional data, derive meaningful business metrics, and present insights through a professional, analyst-level Excel dashboard.

---

## Business Problem

Management lacked a consolidated view of:

* How revenue and sales volume evolved over time
* Which products, countries, and salespeople were driving performance
* Whether performance issues stemmed from pricing, volume, or sales activity

Without a centralized dashboard, identifying growth drivers and inefficiencies required manual reporting and ad hoc analysis.

---

## Dataset Description

* **Source:** Kaggle – Chocolate Sales Dataset
* **Records:** 1,094 transactions
* **Period Covered:** January to August 2022
* **Granularity:** One row per transaction
* **Fields:**

  * Salesperson
  * Country
  * Product
  * Date
  * Amount (Revenue)
  * Products Shipped (Boxes)

The dataset was clean, with no missing values. Repeated salesperson names represent multiple valid transactions.

## Key Metrics (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue:** $6,183,625
* **Total Boxes Shipped:** 177,007
* **Total Transactions:** 1,094
* **Average Revenue per Box:** $34.19

These KPIs provide a high-level snapshot of overall business performance and update dynamically with dashboard filters.

---

## Dashboard Structure

The Excel dashboard consolidates all analysis into a single interactive view:

* KPI cards summarizing overall performance
* Monthly revenue trend analysis
* Monthly shipment volume with month-on-month (MoM) growth
* Revenue breakdown by product
* Revenue breakdown by country
* Revenue contribution by salesperson

Interactive slicers allow users to filter results by period, country, product, and salesperson, enabling flexible and self-service analysis.

---

## Key Insights

* The business generated **$6.18M in revenue** from **177,007 boxes** over eight months, indicating strong overall demand.
* Shipment volumes peaked early in the year, with mid-year recovery followed by a decline toward August, suggesting potential seasonality or demand softening.
* A small group of products accounts for a disproportionate share of revenue, with the top five products contributing **27.81%** of total sales.
* Revenue is relatively balanced across countries, with Australia, the UK, and India emerging as the strongest markets.
* Sales performance is concentrated among top performers: the top 20% of salespeople generated **25.52%** of total revenue, highlighting both strength and dependency risk.
